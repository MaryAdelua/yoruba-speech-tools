All AI recordings are longer than their natural counterpart: 1.10–2.00 times total duration. This includes leading/trailing audio and does not prove segment stretching or incorrect rhythm.
Counterexample: noticeably-unnatural takes have median duration ratio 1.845, versus 1.378 for very-unnatural takes. Greater duration does not monotonically track worse overall judgment.
Across all takes, pYIN–Praat median pitch disagreement is 0.139–0.348 semitones despite poor perceptual ratings. Agreement helps assess tracking consistency, not linguistic correctness.
Coarse relative-pitch difference medians are 2.091 versus 2.267 semitones for noticeably/very unnatural. These overlapping small-sample summaries do not establish separation. Ten normalized-duration bins do not align syllables; speech onset/offset and content timing can dominate differences. Raw common-bin coverage is 6–9/10; screened coverage 4–8/10.
Within p02 and p06, take 2 is rated very unnatural versus noticeably unnatural for take 1, and its raw relative-pitch distribution is wider. However both take-2 recordings reach the pYIN search floor. This is a candidate for manual tracker/segment inspection, not evidence that greater pitch range caused unnaturalness.
p04 and p05 have unchanged ratings despite differences in duration and contours. p03 take 2 has less severe segmental rating but a larger coarse contour difference. No single measured distance consistently orders these judgments.
Voicing differences, run duration/count and contour-change descriptors remain contaminated by pitch detection failures. Unvoiced runs can include consonants or silence; they are not verified pauses, syllable durations, or speaking rate.

D. DIMENSIONS AND FEATURE GAPS
Lexical tone: needs confirmed tone-bearing units and supervised temporal alignment, reliable local F0 and contextual tone realization. Whole-utterance shape is insufficient; current ratings cannot discriminate severity.
Segmental pronunciation: current F0/duration statistics are insufficient. Need vowel identity/alignment, formant trajectories and consonant-specific acoustic evidence. One singleton rating contrast is not validation.
Nasality: perceptual counts only: normal 1, somewhat excessive/unusual 3, clearly excessive/unusual 8. No F0-to-nasality association calculated. Future A1-P0/A1-P1 spectral relationships, F1 bandwidth, spectral tilt, segment durations/trajectories require segment/vowel context and reliability validation; none is an established Yorùbá nasality score here.
Prosody/intonation: relative contour shape is a plausible candidate, but requires phrase alignment and separation from lexical tones. All ratings currently identical.
Rhythm/fluency: local segment durations, lengthening and verified pause structure are promising next descriptors; total duration alone is not supported as a discriminator.
Overall naturalness: preserve direct listener category independently. Multiple perceptual factors and low-confidence, severely imbalanced categories preclude fitting a predictor.

F. DECISION
No feature has yet demonstrated predictive validity. Highest-priority hypotheses: supervised local segment duration/stretching and aligned, speaker-relative F0 trajectories. Estimator disagreement should qualify measurements rather than predict correctness. Raw pitch range and total duration alone are not supported predictors.
The pilot is operationally sufficient to design a small future comparison with another model, but scientifically insufficient to validate an evaluator or claim that gpt-realtime-2.1 or gpt-live-1 will improve Yorùbá. Neither newer model was queried, checked for availability or generated in this run. Do not scale to both at once on this evidence.
Next smallest useful experiment: revisit p01–p03 only: 3 natural clips + 6 existing AI takes (9 recordings), with clarified rating/confidence anchors, randomized presentation, and marked intervals for a few salient events. These cover nasal disagreement, overall-category disagreement and segmental disagreement. Confirm whether LOW means uncertainty in the judgment; never recode silently. Rate natural clips as well so positive examples are observed rather than assumed. Then supervise boundaries and inspect local F0/durations at those intervals. A second listener on this small subset would help distinguish reviewer uncertainty from measurement limitations.
After that check and explicit approval, a single newer model on a few approved sentences with two repeats is a bounded next generation experiment. No new models or audio added now.

Exploratory v2 findings

Only the completed natural and paired v2 reviews were used. All six natural references passed transcript matching in natural QC v2. This is not a pronunciation gold standard.

All 12 AI takes were judged clearly problematic for lexical tone, segmental pronunciation, prosody/intonation and rhythm/fluency; all were very unnatural overall, with high confidence. These dimensions have no category variation, so no feature can be validated as predicting differences in these judgments in this sample.

Perceived nasality: 3 normal, 3 somewhat excessive/unusual, 6 clearly excessive/unusual. Repeats differ in nasality for p01, p03 and p06 and agree for p02, p04 and p05. All other rating categories agree within sentence pairs. Nasal-quality tags were selected on 11 takes, including some rated normal; retain both fields without silently reconciling them. No F0-based nasality inference was performed.

All AI files are longer than their natural references (1.10–2.00 times duration). File duration includes silence and is not speaking rate. Coarse relative-contour differences span about 1.16–3.72 semitones; bins are based on fractional file duration, not aligned syllables. These are descriptive differences, not errors. Relative pitch preserves raw F0 and uses the existing own-utterance normalization; it does not remove all speaker, context or timing effects.

AI pYIN/Praat median absolute disagreement on jointly voiced frames spans 0.14–0.35 semitones, while voicing disagreement spans 11.49–23.17 percent. Low median pitch disagreement does not establish reliability of every frame. Large pitch-width differences (notably p02 repeat 2 and p06 repeat 2) need inspection against frame flags and contours before interpretation.

Most useful next measurements: supervised segment/syllable durations and aligned relative-pitch trajectories. No feature is currently shown to predict the listener judgments. Segmental pronunciation needs phonetic boundaries and vowel/consonant spectral features. Nasality needs exploratory A1-P0/A1-P1 relationships, F1 bandwidth, spectral tilt and segment trajectories, with channel/speaker controls and native interval labels. These are not yet validated Yoruba nasality measures.

Decision: the pilot demonstrates a reproducible workflow and motivates a bounded model comparison, but does not validate an automatic evaluator or establish a ranking. Smallest next experiment: supervise boundaries and native problem intervals for one existing sentence with both AI repeats, contrasting it with its natural reference. Then, after approval and verification of model/audio availability, add one requested model at a time on the same six texts. No newer model was added in this run.

All original notes, tags, intervals and confidence are preserved in the joined outputs. No training, combined score, final correctness score, acoustic re-extraction or new audio generation occurred.

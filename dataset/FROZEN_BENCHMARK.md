# Frozen benchmark definition

## Freeze identifier

`yoruba-tone-calibration-0.1-research`

The 120 prompt IDs, transcripts, English meanings, contrast groups, and ordinary evaluation criteria are frozen at this checkpoint. Prompt-level intervention results are retained as completed evidence and must not be used to rewrite test items.

## Development boundary

- training/development data may be extended separately;
- the controlled benchmark items must not be optimized against repeatedly;
- future architecture-level experiments must declare any benchmark overlap;
- model selection and final reporting must use predeclared metrics;
- `0163` remains marked as technically unusable only for the earlier ChatGPT baseline capture, not as an invalid dataset item.

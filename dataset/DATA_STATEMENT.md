# Data statement

## Speaker

One adult fluent Yoruba speaker recorded the corpus. Privacy-sensitive demographic details are not stored in this public-facing metadata checkpoint. The speaker described the target variety as fluent Yoruba rather than a dialect- or region-specific collection.

## Speech collection

The speaker read controlled prompts one recording at a time using a personal computer and Windows Sound Recorder. Recordings were standardized to mono 24 kHz PCM, checked automatically, and reviewed by the speaker for transcript correctness and naturalness.

## Text design

Prompts were designed to cover Yoruba vowels, underdotted letters, lexical tones, connected speech, questions, negation, focus, commands, and 17 meaning-relevant contrast groups. English meanings are supplied to support meaning-recovery evaluation.

## Annotation process

Orthographic tone labels are derived from Unicode diacritics and represented as H, M, and L. The fluent speaker verified all 120 utterances. Automatic F0 analysis is diagnostic and is not treated as a substitute for human lexical-tone judgment.

## External validity

The corpus is a controlled calibration resource. It does not sample the full social, geographic, dialectal, or prosodic diversity of Yoruba. Claims based on it must be limited accordingly.

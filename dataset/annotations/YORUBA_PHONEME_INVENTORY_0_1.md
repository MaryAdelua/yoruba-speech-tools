# Yoruba broad phoneme inventory 0.1

This inventory is an automatic, orthography-derived representation for model integration. It must receive linguistic and fluent-speaker review before being labeled a canonical pronunciation dictionary.

## Vowels

`a /a/`, `e /e/`, `ẹ /ɛ/`, `i /i/`, `o /o/`, `ọ /ɔ/`, `u /u/`.

Acute and grave accents provide orthographic tone targets but do not change the segmental vowel symbol. Nasal realization is not yet encoded as a settled phoneme feature; vowel-plus-`n` sequences are routed to review.

## Consonants

`b /b/`, `d /d/`, `f /f/`, `g /ɡ/`, `gb /ɡ͡b/`, `h /h/`, `j /dʒ/`, `k /k/`, `l /l/`, `m /m/`, `n /n/`, `p /k͡p/`, `r /ɾ/`, `s /s/`, `ṣ /ʃ/`, `t /t/`, `w /w/`, `y /j/`.

## Tone

Acute = `H`, unmarked = `M`, grave = `L`. These are lexical/orthographic labels. Contextual surface tone, downstep, assimilation, and elision remain separate annotation layers.

## Known unresolved cases

- vowel-plus-`n` sequences and nasalization;
- syllabic nasals and their tones;
- compounds and hyphen boundaries;
- loanword-specific variants;
- connected-speech deletion, assimilation, and surface tone;
- narrow phonetic variation across speakers and contexts.
